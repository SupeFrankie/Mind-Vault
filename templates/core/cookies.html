1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website asks your browser to store. They let a site remember things between page loads, such as the fact that you are signed in.

2. Cookies MindVault uses

MindVault uses only three cookies, all set by its web framework (Django) to keep the site working and secure. All three are essential, which is why MindVault does not show a cookie consent banner: there are no optional cookies to accept or refuse.

3. Browser storage that is not a cookie

MindVault also saves two display preferences in your browser's local storage. Local storage is separate from cookies, stays on your device, is not sent to MindVault's server and does not identify you. A value is only saved after you use the matching control.

KeyPurposeValues
mindvault-themeRemembers whether you chose light or dark mode.light or dark
mindvault-sidebar-collapsedRemembers whether you collapsed the side navigation.true or false

These stay until you clear your browser's site data.

4. Analytics, advertising and third parties

  • MindVault uses no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies or scripts.
  • MindVault sets no cookies on behalf of third parties.
  • Pages load fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). That request sends your IP address and browser details to Google, as described in the Privacy Policy. Google's own policies cover what it does with that data.
  • Google, Apple and school-email sign-in are "coming soon" placeholders and do not contact those services or set their cookies.

If optional cookies or analytics are added in future, MindVault will ask for your consent before they are used and will update this page first.

5. Managing cookies and storage

  • Your browser's settings let you view, block and delete cookies and site data for MindVault.
  • If you block or delete the essential cookies, you will be signed out and forms (including sign-in) will stop working, and confirmation messages may not appear.
  • Clearing site data also resets the two display preferences above.
